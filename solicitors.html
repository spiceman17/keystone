THE COMMERCIAL MODEL

We help take care of the operational costs.

Running a legal practice comes with significant ongoing costs. Our model is designed to help take some of that burden away. As part of the platform arrangement, the company covers agreed insurance, ongoing training and other eligible operational costs required for solicitors operating through the platform.

Rather than managing and funding these costs separately, they are incorporated into our commercial model.

In return, the company takes an agreed percentage of earnings from eligible work processed through the platform to contribute towards the ongoing insurance, training, compliance, technology and operational support provided.

You focus on the legal work. We help take care of the infrastructure.

The exact percentage and costs covered will be detailed in the solicitor agreement.

REFERRAL FEE ARRANGEMENT

An agreed referral fee for introduced work

As part of joining our solicitor network, firms must be able to offer an agreed referral fee to participating estate agents for eligible work introduced through the platform, subject to applicable laws, regulations, professional rules and our terms and conditions.

This helps create a sustainable relationship between the solicitor and estate agent while providing potential opportunities for both parties.
